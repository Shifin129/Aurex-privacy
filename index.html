Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AurexMc Official ("Aurex", "Bot", or "Service") handles information when you use the Aurex Discord application.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Aurex may process basic Discord information such as your Discord user ID, username, server ID, channel ID, role information, and information required to operate commands and server features.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide, maintain, secure, and improve Aurex features, respond to commands, manage server configuration, and prevent abuse.

3. Messages and Content

If a feature requires message content, Aurex may process the relevant content while performing that feature. Aurex does not intentionally collect or store message content unless a feature specifically requires it.

4. Data Storage

Some configuration or operational data may be stored to keep Aurex features working across restarts. We aim to retain only information that is reasonably necessary for the Service.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate Aurex, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the Service.

6. Third-Party Services

Aurex operates through Discord and may use hosting, database, monitoring, or other infrastructure providers. These services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by Aurex. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant feature or operational purpose. If you have a privacy or deletion request, contact the Aurex development team through the official Aurex Discord server.

9. Children's Privacy

Aurex does not knowingly request personal information from children outside the requirements and protections applicable to Discord and relevant law.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Aurex development team through the official Aurex Discord server.
